Box plot whiskers: Tukey, Spear and Altman

Whiskers do not have one universal meaning. BoxPlotR supports three definitions, so every figure legend should state which one was used.

Create a box plot

Compare the definitions

Tukey whiskers

The default. Whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5 times the interquartile range from the lower and upper quartiles. Values beyond them are shown individually as outliers.

Spear whiskers

Whiskers extend to the observed minimum and maximum. No observations fall beyond the whiskers, so extreme values are less visually distinct.

Altman whiskers

Whiskers represent the 5th and 95th percentiles. BoxPlotR recommends this definition only for sufficiently large samples, traditionally more than 40 observations.

How to choose

Use Tukey whiskers for a familiar exploratory summary and explicit display of observations beyond the 1.5×IQR fences. Use minimum-to-maximum whiskers when the full observed range is the intended statistic. Use percentile whiskers when a percentile interval is meaningful and the sample is large enough to estimate it reliably.

An observation beyond a Tukey whisker is not automatically erroneous or statistically significant. Investigate the measurement and its context rather than deleting it solely because it appears beyond a whisker.

Suggested legend wording

Centre lines show medians; boxes indicate the 25th and 75th percentiles; whiskers extend to the most extreme observations within 1.5× the interquartile range; points beyond the whiskers are plotted individually.
