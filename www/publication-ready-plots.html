Editable and transparent artwork

For PDF or SVG downloads, enable Transparent background to remove the page and panel colour. Enable Unfilled shapes to retain outlines, whiskers, medians and points while leaving distribution areas ready for recolouring.

Use informative axis labels, report sample sizes, explain whisker definitions, and show raw observations when they materially help readers interpret the data. Avoid choosing a journal-inspired style as a substitute for the target journal's current artwork requirements.

Reproducible figure preparation

  1. Keep the source CSV or Excel file with the analysis.
  2. Download the plotted data and record the selected whisker definition.
  3. Prefer vector output until the final submission format is known.
  4. State in the legend what the centre, box, whiskers, points and mean markers represent.
